WHAT EACH COMPONENT DOES
Browser: shows incidents/evidence and accepts a human decision.
FastAPI: authenticates the user, enforces permissions and creates the work.
Worker/LangGraph: performs the multi-step investigation in the background.
CRM service: holds the authoritative subscription plan.
Support service: holds the account that may need correcting.
PostgreSQL: stores observations, proposal, approval, repair intent and audit.
Executor: rechecks freshness/approval, sends a conditional idempotent update.
Verifier: re-reads records to decide whether the repair actually worked.
Local AI: receives required account evidence, may request additional read tools,
and proposes an explanation. Deterministic policy controls whether a repair is
allowed; the model cannot authorize or choose the final write value.
The local model can make mistakes. If it fails or reaches a budget limit, the
system escalates without proposing a repair. A successful demo does not prove
general model reliability. Read the observations and approval carefully.

WHY HUMAN APPROVAL MATTERS
An AI answer is not permission to change an account. The approved proposal fixes
the exact account, field, value and observed versions. If data changes before the
write, stale checks should reject it. Retrying uses the same operation identity
so a lost response does not cause another mutation.
